Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Duplicate Line Remover handle large lists of text?

Our duplicate line remover splits text into individual lines and utilizes an in-memory Set data structure, allowing it to de-duplicate tens of thousands of lines in milliseconds with O(N) algorithmic efficiency.

How are reading time and speaking time estimated?

Reading time is calculated using the international standard average of 200 words per minute (WPM) for silent reading, while speaking time is calculated at 130 WPM for natural speech cadence.

Can I extract all email addresses or URLs from messy text?

Yes. Simply paste your text and click "Ext. Emails" or "Ext. URLs". The tool executes regex pattern matching and presents all unique extracted matches in a clean modal ready to copy.

Is my text private when using Online Text Tools?

Yes, completely private. All string operations, regex parsing, and frequency counting happen inside your browser DOM. Your text is never uploaded, logged, or analyzed on any external server.
